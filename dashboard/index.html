The farm verdict

The airdrop pool is taken as 10% of the implied value, shared pro-rata across TVL, so a $1,000 ticket earns implied × 0.10 × 1,000 / TVL. Gas is priced per transaction by the protocol's main chain (Ethereum $12, Base/Arbitrum/Optimism/Blast $1, Solana/Polygon/Sui/Aptos $0.10, BNB/Avalanche/Fantom/Tron $0.50, anything else $2), named with the chain's gas token where it is a settled fact (ETH, SOL, POL, SUI, APT, BNB, AVAX, FTM, TRX, HYPE, MON), and multiplied by the plan's transaction count: setup, one a week through the hold, and the claim. FARM if the net is $25 or more, THIN if it is $0 or more, SKIP if the drop does not cover the gas.

Out-of-sample proof

CheckThis boardFlat multiple

Leave-one-out refits the model once per traded token, holding one out each time. Walk-forward fits only the older half of the dated traded tokens and tests the newer half. The flat multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the traded tokens, the simplest rule that prices on TVL alone. The curve beats it on both checks, and on the share of tokens predicted within 3×.

Tenure, tested and not priced

How long a protocol has run with no token was tested as a term in the value model in five forms, each fitted on the residual after curve, category and growth, its contribution clamped to ±0.15 like growth, undated protocols at zero. None of them earns its place:

Every form worsens leave-one-out. The slope's sign flips across forms (−0.03 uncapped, +0.22 capped at 365 days, −0.17 capped at 730), and the capped-365 slope is carried by 8 young protocols: noise, not signal. The one form whose walk-forward median is a touch better (buckets) has near-zero, non-monotonic offsets and a worse within-3× share. So the model does not price age. Age still matters in this board: it sets each farm plan's hold length, which is a different question from what a launch is worth.
